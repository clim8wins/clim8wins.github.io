Singapore has launched a S$143 million (about US$112 million) fund for industrial decarbonisation research, which its Economic Development Board and Advanced Energy and Decarbonisation Office describe as the country's largest single initiative of its kind. Up to S$100 million goes to a new programme, DRIVE, where companies and start-ups team up with public research institutions and the government co-funds up to 75% of qualifying costs. Research areas include capturing carbon dioxide, turning it into products and electrifying industrial heat, in a country where industry produces nearly two-thirds of emissions. Caveats: this funds research rather than cutting emissions directly, and the "largest" claim is the agencies' own. Still, backing the technologies that could clean up heavy industry is a step for the climate.
