Maryland just signed a 20-year deal to build its largest-ever solar farm — on a reclaimed coal mine in Garrett County. The Jade Meadow III project is expected to save the state up to $515 million over two decades while doubling the renewable power Maryland's government buys directly, all in service of Governor Wes Moore's goal of 100% clean energy by 2035. Construction alone is projected to create over 1,200 local jobs.
