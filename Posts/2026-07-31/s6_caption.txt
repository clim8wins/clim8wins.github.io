South Korea's offshore wind sector just took a real step forward. On July 9, Pacifico Energy Korea signed supply-chain agreements with EEW KHPC, KMC Line, and CS Energy to build out its 3.2 GW Jindo offshore wind cluster in Gwangyang Bay, locking in foundation manufacturing, marine logistics, and installation support. In the same window, Hanwha Ocean began physical construction on its separate Shinan-ui offshore wind project. Supply chains closing and construction starting in the same market, at the same time, is a real signal — not just another announcement.
