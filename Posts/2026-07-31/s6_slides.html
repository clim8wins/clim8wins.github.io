THE UPDATE

South Korea's 3.2 GW wind project advances.

On July 9, Pacifico Energy Korea signed supply-chain agreements with EEW KHPC, KMC Line, and CS Energy to build out its 3.2 GW Jindo offshore wind cluster in Gwangyang Bay — locking in foundation manufacturing, marine logistics, and installation support. The same week, Hanwha Ocean began physical construction on its separate Shinan-ui offshore wind project, also in South Korea. Two major projects moving from planning into build-out in the same market, in the same window, signals real momentum in a sector that has historically moved slowly.
