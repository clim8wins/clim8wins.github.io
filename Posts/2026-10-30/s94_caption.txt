A new report finds 30 European industrial clusters could switch to geothermal heat. The nonprofit Project InnerSpace and the think tank Future Cleantech Architects screened EU industrial sites and picked 30 priority clusters in 11 countries, carrying about 2 GW of heat demand, where factories could share geothermal wells. They estimate it could displace over €850 million a year in fuel spending and avoid about 4.3 million tonnes of CO₂, roughly 1% of EU industrial-heat emissions. Caveats: these are the authors' estimates of potential rather than built projects, the report was funded through Project InnerSpace, and the EU's geothermal action plan is still unpublished. Still, using Europe's underground heat instead of imported gas in factories is a step for the climate.
