Tata Power Renewable Energy announced on November 16 that it has commissioned its 100.8 MW Ausa wind project in Latur district, Maharashtra, India. The 28 turbines will supply Tata Power's Mumbai distribution business, and the company estimates about 308 million units of electricity a year and about 252,560 tonnes of CO₂ avoided a year. India's installed wind capacity reached about 59.2 GW in September, up 11% in a year, according to the government's renewable energy ministry. The figures are the company's own estimates, the coverage repeats its announcement, and the contract terms, cost and exact commissioning date aren't given. Still, another wind farm switched on in India is a step for the climate, though its output is still an estimate.
