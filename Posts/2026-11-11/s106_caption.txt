Lenders have backed about US$563 million of wind, solar and battery projects in Egypt. Alcazar Energy Partners reached financial close on two 250 MW wind farms near Ras Ghareb, with US$436 million of debt from the EBRD, the EIB and Germany's DEG, and the EBRD and DEG are providing up to US$127 million for a 258.5 MWp solar plant with a 120 MWh battery at Benban. Both have 25-year power purchase agreements with the state grid company, and the wind farms are due online by the end of 2028. Gas supplied about 80% of Egypt's electricity in 2025 and renewables about 13%, against a target of 42% by 2030, per Ember data. These projects are not built yet, no date is given for Benban, and the homes and CO₂ figures are estimates by the lenders and developers. Still, wind, solar and storage financed on a gas-dependent grid is a step for the climate, once it's built.
