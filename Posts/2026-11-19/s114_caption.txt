Ford announced on November 18 that its BlueOval Battery Park plant in Marshall, Michigan has begun shipping its first lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery cells, bound for Fathom electric pickup prototypes at its Louisville plant. Ford owns and runs the plant but licenses the technology from China's CATL, a reliance the US Transportation Secretary criticised in a September letter that Ford rejected. Ford's goal is a Fathom under US$30,000, with a base price of US$28,350, launching in 2027. These are prototype cells, the range and battery size aren't disclosed, the plant's capacity and jobs targets have already been cut, and no emissions figure has been published. Still, cheaper EV batteries made in the US are a step for the climate, if the pickup arrives at that price.
