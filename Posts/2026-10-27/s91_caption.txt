Two Nordic funds are investing in a 500 MWp solar project in the Philippines. Norway's Climate Investment Fund, managed by Norfund, and Impact Fund Denmark have signed to take a combined 49% stake in Vena Energy's Ixus Bugallon project in Pangasinan, which is under construction after about $310 million of bank loans closed in July. The sponsors expect it to avoid about 530,000 tonnes of CO₂ a year and match the power use of 650,000 Philippine households, in a country where coal supplies about 62% of electricity. Caveats: the stake is subject to Philippine Competition Commission approval, the price is undisclosed, and the figures are projections. Still, public and private money flowing into solar to displace coal is a step for the climate.
