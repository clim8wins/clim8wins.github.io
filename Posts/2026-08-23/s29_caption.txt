Rooftop solar covers over a third of Australian homes — but batteries are what let all that midday sunshine power evenings too. Australia just passed 500,000 home batteries installed under its federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program, storing roughly 14 gigawatt-hours that charge from cheap solar by day and discharge through the evening peak. The effect is already visible at the grid level: AEMO's latest quarterly report found wholesale electricity prices fell 47% year-on-year to $74/MWh, the lowest June-quarter average since 2020, as batteries increasingly take gas's place.
