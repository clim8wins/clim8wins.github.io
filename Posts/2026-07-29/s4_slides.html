THE UPDATE

US grid saved $170 million during heatwave.

During July 2026's record heatwave, wind and solar generation actually increased during the highest-demand hours — helping save the grid more than $170 million in wholesale electricity costs, according to the American Clean Power Association. The same analysis found that 74 GW of solar, wind, and battery storage projects already waiting to connect to four major grid operators would have delivered even more savings and prevented modeled reliability shortfalls, had they been online in time.
