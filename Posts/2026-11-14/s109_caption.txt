In its Renewables 2026 report, published on November 6, the International Energy Agency forecasts almost 4,800 GW of new renewable capacity worldwide between 2026 and 2031, about 57% more than in the previous six years. Solar PV is about three quarters of it, and for the first time additions in emerging and developing economies outside China are expected to match those in advanced economies. This is a forecast, not built capacity: yearly additions are expected to fall 11% in 2026, grid connection queues have grown to 3,000 GW, and the IEA says the 2030 total still falls short of the COP28 goal of tripling renewables. Still, a forecast of record growth spreading to more countries is a step for the climate.
