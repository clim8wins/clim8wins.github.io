The world just crossed 3 terawatts of installed solar power — and the pace keeps accelerating. The first terawatt took nearly 68 years to build, the second about two years, and the third just 19 months. The world added 664 gigawatts of new solar in 2025 alone, and in April 2026 wind and solar combined generated more electricity than natural gas globally for the first time ever, according to Ember. China now installs more than half the world's solar, driving prices down far enough that Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines, and Cuba are seeing their own rapid solar booms — the number of countries with at least 1 gigawatt of solar has grown from 42 in 2020 to 74 today.
