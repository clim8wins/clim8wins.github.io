Türkiye's COP31 Presidency has launched the Antalya Compact for Energy Efficient Buildings, a framework governments can endorse before the climate summit opens in Antalya on November 9. It aims to cut the energy intensity of the world's buildings by at least 25% by 2035, in a sector behind about 37% of global CO₂ emissions. No endorsements have been reported yet, the target's baseline year differs between sources, and building emissions rose 1% in 2024. Still, a global push to cut building energy use is a step for the climate.
