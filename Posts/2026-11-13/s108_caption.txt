Abu Dhabi's Masdar says its Lockhart Solar and Storage complex in San Bernardino County, California, is complete, with 365 MW of solar and 173.7 MW of battery storage. The final 80 MW phase reached commercial operation in July, according to Terra-Gen, the developer, which Masdar owns jointly with Igneo Infrastructure Partners. Masdar says the complex can power about 350,000 homes and businesses a year and puts its cost at US$950 million, with power sold under long-term agreements with California utilities and corporate buyers. Every figure here is the owners' own: the homes estimate has no stated basis, the buyers and the battery's energy capacity aren't named, no emissions estimate has been published, and the final phase came online four months ago. Still, solar and batteries delivering power at scale is a step for the climate.
