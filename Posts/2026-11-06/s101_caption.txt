The World Health Organization says scaling up six climate-and-health actions could prevent about 1.6 million deaths a year and avoid about 16.7 billion tonnes of CO₂, equal to 31% of annual global greenhouse gas emissions excluding land use. The actions are heat-health warning systems, climate-resilient drinking water, basic sanitation, handwashing facilities, cleaner household fuels and efficient fossil fuel pricing, and WHO estimates every US$1 invested in five of them returns about US$24. These are modelled scale-up scenarios, not results: WHO calls them aspirational, some actions were modelled without their synergies, and the return figure leaves out fossil fuel pricing. Still, health and climate gains that reinforce each other are a step for the climate.
