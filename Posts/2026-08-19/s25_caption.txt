California generated 51% of its electricity from solar in May 2026 — up from 45.2% a year earlier and just 17% a decade ago — making it the first major economy in the world to cross 50% solar for a full month, according to energy think tank Ember. The record was made possible by battery storage, which grew from 61 megawatts in 2016 to 16 gigawatts today. Batteries now commonly supply more than a quarter of the state's electricity demand for parts of the night, shifting abundant midday solar into the evening. Solar has outproduced natural gas in California every month of 2026 so far, beating it on a daily basis 82% of the time between January and May — up from just 21% over the same months a year earlier.
