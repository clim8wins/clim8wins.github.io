China's clean-tech exports are on track to cut other countries' emissions by about 2% in 2026, according to a Carbon Brief analysis by Lauri Myllyvirta of the Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air. Exports of solar panels, wind turbines, EVs and batteries are estimated to avoid about 450 million tonnes of CO₂ a year, about Brazil's annual fossil emissions and up from roughly 370 million in 2025. About 76% of the lifetime benefit lands in the global South. Caveats: it is a modelled estimate, it assumes the exports replace fossil power and fuel abroad, the final quarter of 2026 is projected, and the avoided emissions are small next to global totals. Still, clean technology that cuts emissions well beyond the country making it is a step for the climate.
