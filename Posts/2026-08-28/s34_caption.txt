Africa is on track to install a record 17 gigawatts of solar in 2026, up 45% from last year — the continent's third consecutive annual record, with 36 of its 54 countries expected to hit their highest-ever installations. Growth is no longer just South Africa's story: its share of the continent's solar has fallen from over half historically to under 20% this year, as the boom spreads elsewhere — the Democratic Republic of Congo is forecast to grow 544%, Zimbabwe 282%, and Egypt 176%. Much of this growth is distributed rooftop and off-grid solar, driven by falling panel prices, unreliable centralized grids, and pay-as-you-go financing — and it's so decentralized that official statistics barely capture it.
