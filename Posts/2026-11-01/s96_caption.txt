The world is now restoring about 252 million hectares of degraded land, rivers, wetlands and coasts across 149 countries, according to the first global progress report from UNEP, FAO and the CBD Secretariat. It's the largest documented restoration effort to date, with more than one million green jobs created. The report also says the world is off track: that's 34% of the 2030 benchmark, with land nearly half-way but marine and coastal restoration at 23% and freshwater at 5%. Restoring nature can pull carbon from the air and protect wildlife, a win-win for the climate.
