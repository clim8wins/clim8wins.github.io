US voters changed control of Congress in the November 3 midterms, with Democrats winning both the Senate and the House according to NPR's analysis of Associated Press race calls. Climate coverage points to what could follow: a say over must-pass funding bills that could revive frozen programmes such as the EPA's US$7 billion community solar programme, and full Democratic control of state governments in Wisconsin, Arizona and Pennsylvania. Voters also passed transit measures in the Bay Area, Colorado, Seattle and Lawrence, Kansas. None of the policy changes has happened yet: the president can veto, some counts are still being finalised, and funding bills need some Republican votes. Still, a shift in who controls climate funding is a possible step for the climate, if it's delivered.
