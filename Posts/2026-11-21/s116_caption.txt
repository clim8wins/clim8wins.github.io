A scorecard from the think tank IEEFA and the consultancy Green Energy Markets says 2.4 GW of large-scale solar, wind and battery projects were committed to construction in Australia in July to September, an all-time quarterly record and 20% above the previous peak in early 2022. Wind led with 1.5 GW and solar added 910 MW, and projects in construction represent about 21,600 job-years of work. Queensland's gap to its 2030 requirements is 90% bridged, according to Green Energy Markets, while New South Wales lags. This is construction committed, not power flowing: only about 6% of fully approved projects have a power contract, and BloombergNEF's outlook has Australia at 61–64% renewables by 2030, short of the 82% goal. Still, a record for projects reaching construction is a step for the climate, though the build-out is still short of the 2030 goal.
