US solar just outgenerated both coal and wind for the first time in a single month. EIA data for May 2026 shows solar produced 47,147 GWh, ahead of coal's 45,119 GWh and wind's 41,157 GWh. Across the first five months of 2026, wind and solar combined produced 57% more electricity than coal, with renewables accounting for over 30% of total US generation.
