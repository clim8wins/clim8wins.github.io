Brazil's Atlantic Forest just hit its lowest deforestation level in 40 years. New monitoring from SOS Mata Atlântica found the biome lost just 8,658 hectares in 2025 — down 40% from 2024, and the first time annual deforestation has dropped below 10,000 hectares since record-keeping began in 1985. If the trend holds, conservationists say the forest could reach zero deforestation within three years.
