Shanghai-based Envision Energy says its green ammonia has been supplied to bunker the Terra Clove, a 93,000 m³ very large ammonia carrier that Eastern Pacific Shipping named at HD Hyundai Heavy Industries in Ulsan, South Korea, on October 22. Ammonia contains no carbon, so burning it releases no CO₂ at the point of use, and made with renewable power it could displace the fossil fuels behind shipping's 2–3% of global emissions. This is Envision's own announcement, no bunkering volume was disclosed, and sea trials are still ahead. Most ammonia today is made from methane, so only green ammonia delivers the benefit, and the ship's engine also uses a small amount of diesel pilot fuel. Still, a step for the climate.
