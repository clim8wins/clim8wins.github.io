India has restored nearly 22 million hectares of degraded land — putting it on track to beat its own Bonn Challenge pledge. It's part of a bigger global milestone: a new IUCN report finds 124.3 million hectares are now under restoration worldwide, an area twice the size of France, in the first-ever global count of its kind. Countries have pledged 1.2 billion hectares of restoration by around 2030 — so there's real progress already banked, with more still ahead.
