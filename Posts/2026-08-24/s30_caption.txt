Canada just approved the largest clean energy investment in North American history — a $70 billion package including $10 billion in federal financing to upgrade Churchill Falls and build the new Gull Island hydro project in Labrador. Together they'll nearly triple Churchill Falls' output to 14,000 megawatts, enough to power Toronto, Montréal, and Vancouver combined. The deal also resolves decades of tension between Newfoundland and Labrador and Quebec over the original 1969 Churchill Falls contract, and is expected to support 23,000 jobs and add $31 billion to Canada's GDP through the early 2040s.
